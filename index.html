Who Owns Your Shelf?

What This Is

BackBar Detox is a free tool for hairstylists and beauty professionals to reveal who actually owns the brands on their back bar — corporate investors, large publicly traded companies, or truly independent businesses. Because who profits from your shelf matters.

The Rating System

🚩
Investor Owned — Owned by corporate investors (private equity). Returns for shareholders come before your craft or your clients.
🏢
Publicly Traded — Owned by a public corporation (L'Oréal, Coty, Henkel, Unilever). Quarterly earnings drive decisions.
⚠️
Recently Acquired — Ownership has changed recently. May have been indie when you fell in love with it.
🔵
Large Private — Held by a larger private company or founding family. Not PE-backed, but not truly independent.
✅
Independent — No corporate parent or outside investors found. These brands reinvest in quality and stylist support.
⭐
Indie / Women-Owned — The gold standard. Independently owned and woman-led or B-Corp certified. Every dollar stays in independent hands.

How It Works

Known brands resolve instantly from our curated database of 60+ professional beauty brands with sourced ownership data. Unknown brands are researched in real time via AI. Community voting lets working stylists confirm or flag any rating. No login required — works in any mobile browser.

Data Sources

Ownership data sourced from WWD, Beauty Inc, corporate acquisition records, investor portfolio disclosures, SEC filings, and company press releases 2020–2025. Always verify independently for business decisions.
